About me

I'm a final-year IT student at Belgium Campus iTversity, specialising in Infrastructure and Security. I like taking systems apart to understand how they hold up under pressure — then documenting exactly what I found, clearly enough that someone else could pick it up and act on it.

Alongside cybersecurity, I'm also building toward AI and automation — applying AI tooling to real workflows rather than just the theory. My hands-on experience so far comes from lab environments and project work: cloud security monitoring builds, network hardening exercises, and a virtual cybersecurity internship with MWR CyberSec. I'm currently working toward Microsoft's SC-900 (Security, Compliance, and Identity Fundamentals) and hold an FNB Full Stack Developer certification.

I'm actively looking for opportunities across IT, cybersecurity, and AI/automation — infrastructure, security operations, AI-assisted development, or roles where I can keep building on this foundation.
